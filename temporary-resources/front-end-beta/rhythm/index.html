We Are Not a Gun Club

A member-based network of outdoor sporting clubs, built on the belief that outdoorsmanship is baseline human health.

Firearms, fly fishing, archery, field medical, fitness, cooking, navigation. Not ideology, but capability. Practices so old they feel new. A busy person can arrive, drop in, feel reconnected, and be home within three hours.

We find remarkable properties, make them flourish, and open the gate: creating access to land, elite instruction, and real community for people who would never otherwise have it. All of it delivered with a hospitality mindset. You are not processed at Rhythm Outdoors™. You are received.

The Rules

Three Rules

We unify around practice, not grievance. The whole culture fits on three lines.

Rule · 01

Be a Good Human

Hold the door for others. Help when it gets hard. Address your flaws. Look inward before you blame outwardly. Ego is the enemy, we are all still students.

Rule · 02

No Politics

Doctors shoot next to contractors, cowboys next to hippies, all of them respectfully. Credentials stay at the gate. We unify around shared practice, not grievance or division.

Rule · 03

Be Safe

We teach what good safety looks like. Please be honest about your experience. Some things are worth being inflexible about, and safety is one of them.

Our Services · Under the Rhythm Outdoors™ Mark

What We Offer

Everything below is delivered under the Rhythm Outdoors™ name, and every one of them is open for inquiry today.

Service · Membership

Private Membership, Hospitality-Minded

Membership at Rhythm Outdoors™ clubs includes facility access across disciplines, member gatherings and events, clubhouse amenities, and a community that knows your name. Memberships are available now at all three clubs: individual, household, family, and corporate tiers.

Service · Education & Training

Elite Instruction, Democratized

From Special Forces instructors to World Champions, we make elite knowledge accessible to everyday enthusiasts. Classes, workshops, and progressions in marksmanship, fly casting, wingshooting, sporting clays, precision rifle, archery, field medical, fitness, cooking, navigation, and field craft: what we call uncommon life skills. Some courses are member-only; many are open to the public.

Service · Rhythm Adventures™ · Guided Travel

Concierge Expeditions, Local to International

Rhythm Adventures™ is our travel service: we plan, book, outfit, and guide outdoor expeditions for members and their guests. Destination selection, vetted outfitter partnerships, gear recommendations, skill-readiness training. Every detail handled, from a guided day trip an hour from home to a safari on another continent.

Local

Guided day trips. Weekend getaways. Prime hunting grounds and fishing holes within driving distance.

Regional

Multi-day excursions. Trophy fish on storied rivers. Upland bird hunts. Coastal charters.

National

The salmon runs of Alaska. The elk country of Montana. The redfish flats of Texas.

International

Argentina dove hunting. Mexico offshore fishing. African safari.

The Skill-Ready Guarantee. Before every trip we assess your current skill level and train the gaps, so you arrive at every destination prepared, capable, and confident.

Trips run and in planning: Argentina · Alaska · Texas Coast · Mexico · Africa · and more in development.

Service · Club Retail

Curated Essentials, Nothing More

Lean, intentional retail at each clubhouse: range and field essentials, ammunition for the disciplines we teach, embroidered Rhythm Outdoors™ goods, and a few premium finds that fit the culture. YETI drinkware, Turtle Boxes, gear worth owning.

Programs · Access & Youth

Access for Those Who Serve and Those Who Follow

Service Access. Dedicated, reduced-barrier access for First Responders, Active Military, and Law Enforcement: space to train, decompress, and belong. Scholarships extend to high-school pistol and shotgun teams that train on our grounds; several alumni now shoot for college teams and service academies.

Youth Education. With Alpha School and Waypoint Academy, students finish rigorous academics by late morning and spend afternoons building discipline, marksmanship, archery, and leadership with our instructors. One student at a time, off the iPad and into the field.

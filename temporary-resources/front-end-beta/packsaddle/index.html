Go home better than you came.

Packsaddle is a private mountain in the Texas Hill Country, and a club built around one simple idea: people are happier and more capable when they spend real time outside, getting good at hard things.

We teach those things. Precision rifle, carbine, pistol, fieldcraft, fitness, drone work, land nav, and adventure. There is a serious rifle range here, but the gun was never the point. The point is the skill, the place, and the people you spend time with.

The Mountain

Five disciplines share the mountain.

ELR

Out to ≈3,750 yards, with downrange cameras and electronic hit indicators. Rare in Texas, rare anywhere.

One Mile

Long-range precision and friendly competition. The everyday workhorse.

High Angle

Real elevation. Mountain and hunting simulation you cannot get on flat ground.

Dynamic

Pistol and carbine. Movement, target transitions, and low-light work.

Night

Suppressed, night vision, and thermal. Long range and dynamic work, after dark.

Multi-elevation lanes

Shooting angles and positions simply not possible on a flat range.

Real-world training

High-angle positions, low light, and variable terrain, year round.

Complete seclusion

No public road access, no adjacent development, no crowds.

≈2,000 acres

A private mountain with genuine elevation, rare for Central Texas.

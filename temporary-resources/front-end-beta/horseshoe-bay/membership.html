Premier Amenities

Shotgun Range

Sporting clays course with Hill Country views, plus three shooting decks.

  • 12-station sporting clays course
  • 5-Stand and Flurry decks
  • Helice ring

Pistol Range

Safe, supervised sessions for shooters of all experience levels.

  • One 50-yard pistol bay
  • Three 25-yard pistol bays
  • Covered pavilion with group seating

Members' Lounge

Unwind in the clubhouse or Trophy Room after your time on the range.

  • Clubhouse with retail and food & beverage
  • Trophy Room with game tables and lounge area
  • The Last Shot bar: craft cocktails, six days a week

Club Spotlight

The Last Shot Bar

Nestled inside the Trophy Room, The Last Shot Bar is where members unwind with craft cocktails and cold beer after time on the range. Six days a week, this is your place to decompress, connect, and drink well.

Membership & Eligibility

Are the Sporting Club amenities open to the public? expand_more

No, the Sporting Club is a private, members-only facility. All range and club amenities are restricted to members and their approved guests. Third-party groups may inquire about group events by emailing info@hsbsportingclub.com.

Is membership individual or family-based? expand_more

Membership privileges mirror those of the resort and include the primary member, spouse, and dependents under the age of 26.

Members may designate up to two additional designees (age 21+, not members of the Club at Horseshoe Bay, who reside outside of 25 miles from the club). Individuals must complete a liability release and must be legally permitted to own or possess a firearm.

All Sporting Club members are required to participate in a safety orientation prior to engaging in shooting activities. Schedule your onboarding.

Is there a cart fee? expand_more

Yes. The fee for both the 4-seat and 6-seat golf carts is $15 (plus tax) per person.

Is there a membership cap? expand_more

Membership capacity is currently under review and will be communicated as the club grows.

Are there corporate memberships? expand_more

We do not offer corporate memberships at this time.

Is this part of the Bronco Experience? expand_more

No. The Sporting Club operates independently, in partnership with The Club at Horseshoe Bay.

Billing & Financial Policies

How are charges handled? expand_more

For convenience, all Sporting Club expenses are applied directly through your existing resort account.

Are clay targets and ammunition included in membership fees? expand_more

Clay targets are included with your membership. Ammunition may be purchased at the clubhouse front desk.

Are credit cards accepted? expand_more

Most charges will be applied to membership accounts, with the exception of booking private training and other on-site events and classes, which is done through the club website and will require credit card payment upon registration.

Location & Access

How do I gain access to the Sporting Club? expand_more

Members will be granted gate access upon onboarding at the club.

Can I drive my personal vehicle to the pistol bays? expand_more

Yes. Vehicle access to the pistol bays is permitted in accordance with club guidelines. Members must check in at the clubhouse before entering the range.

Are members allowed on property after business hours? expand_more

No, members will not have access when staff is not present.

Is there a locker room? expand_more

A shower is available for member convenience. Full locker room facilities are not available at this time.

Are children allowed in all areas of the club? expand_more

Anyone under age 18 must be accompanied by a legal guardian at all times.

Are pets allowed in the clubhouse and on the range? expand_more

Yes, dogs are permitted but must be leashed and under the owner's control at all times.

Do you have meeting spaces? expand_more

Yes. The clubhouse features a formal conference room, as well as lounge and game room spaces suitable for informal meetings, in addition to private dining areas.

Food & Beverage

What food and beverages are offered? expand_more

Grab-and-go items and beverages are available for purchase in the clubhouse. The clubhouse also features a complimentary espresso bar. Our full bar, The Last Shot, located in the Trophy Room, serves craft cocktails and beer.

Do you offer catering? expand_more

Catering is available through the resort and can be arranged by contacting the club concierge.

What is the club's alcohol policy? expand_more

No alcohol or mind-altering substances, including medications that impair judgment or coordination, may be consumed before or during shooting activities. Outside alcohol is not permitted at the club. The club reserves the right to prohibit participation in shooting by anyone who appears impaired. The club maintains a zero-tolerance alcohol policy.

Guest Policies & Access

Are there guest fees? expand_more

Yes, there is an $85 per person guest fee. All guest charges (including food and beverage and retail), whether for members of the Club at Horseshoe Bay or non-members, will be applied to the host's Sporting Club member account. Cart usage for guests is included in the fee. Junior guests (age 15 and younger) enjoy a $55 guest fee that includes clays and cart fee.

Is there a limit to the number of guests I may bring? expand_more

Members may bring up to five guests at a time. Groups larger than six require approval from the General Manager.

How many times may an individual visit the Sporting Club as a guest? expand_more

Guests may visit the club no more than three times per year.

Shooting Disciplines & Facilities

What shooting activities are offered? expand_more

The club features 12 covered sporting clays stations, one flurry deck, two shooting decks (5 stand, knockout, and other shotgun games), one Helice ring, and 4 pistol and carbine shooting bays (9mm to 5.56).

Do you offer skeet and trap? expand_more

Skeet and trap fields are not part of the initial launch phase but may be considered in future development phases.

Will there be long-range shooting opportunities? expand_more

Long-range shooting is not available on-site. Packsaddle Precision, a new long-range shooting facility located 15 minutes west of the club, will open in 2026. Sporting Club members will have first access to join at a discounted rate.

Range Operations & Rules

What are the age requirements to shoot on the range? expand_more

Anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a legal guardian.

Can members request specific bay setups? expand_more

Yes, with 24-hour notice. Setups can be requested by calling the club concierge line.

Can I reserve pistol bays? expand_more

While not required, pistol bays are available to reserve by calling or emailing the club, or visiting the concierge desk.

What rifle calibers are permitted in the bays? expand_more

The maximum caliber permitted is 5.56 / 300 Blackout.

Can I zero in my rifle on the range? expand_more

Yes, up to 50 yards.

What types of targets are available on the pistol bays? expand_more

Paper targets, static steel, and moving steel targets are available.

What shotgun loads are allowed? expand_more

Lead shot only, with no sizes larger than 7.5, 8, and 9.

Are there any barrel restrictions on shotguns? expand_more

For clay target shooting the minimum barrel length is 24 inches. Shorter barrels may be used on the pistol bays.

How many throwers are at each sporting clays station, and how often are they rotated? expand_more

For each station there are 4 throwers. Target presentations are rotated regularly.

Does the club accept course-setting requests? expand_more

Yes, requests can be made directly to the General Manager by email or in person.

Instruction, Training & Classes

Do you offer private and group instruction? expand_more

Yes, private instruction and group classes are available for shotgun, pistol, and carbine disciplines.

Will you offer concealed carry classes? expand_more

Yes, members will be able to schedule private concealed carry classes through the club website.

Retail, Rentals & Storage

Can I buy ammunition and gear on-site? expand_more

Yes, ammunition is available for purchase. A curated selection of retail items is available in the pro shop.

What firearms are available for rent, and in which gauges? expand_more

The club offers a selection of shotguns, pistols, and rifles available to rent. See the concierge desk for details.

Do you sell firearms? expand_more

No, firearms are not for sale through the club.

Do you offer storage for members' firearms and ammunition? expand_more

No, not at this time.

Do members receive their 20% savings on retail, food, and beverage? expand_more

Yes, these items are priced with the Club at Horseshoe Bay members' discount in place.

Events, Leagues & Tournaments

Are leagues, tournaments, and social events offered? expand_more

Yes. The club offers a range of events including league nights, club tournaments, and exclusive Members' Dinners. All events are posted on the club website's event calendar page.

Will there be NSCA-registered events? expand_more

No, registered NSCA shoots are not currently planned.

How do I plan an event? expand_more

All events can be scheduled and coordinated directly through the club concierge or General Manager.

Hunting & Experiences

Are hunting experiences offered? expand_more

Yes. The club will offer wing-shooting training as well as guided hunting experiences at select domestic and international locations. Our hunting program will launch soon.

Safety & Emergency Procedures

What are the shooting rules? expand_more

All members must follow strict safety protocols, including firearm handling standards, range etiquette, and caliber restrictions. Complete rules are provided in the Rules section of the Welcome Guide.

Is there an emergency plan in place? expand_more

Yes. Staff are trained to respond to emergencies, contact emergency services, and provide critical information. Emergency procedures are posted throughout the range and clubhouse area.

Property & Team

How large is the property? expand_more

The club spans over 150 acres, encompassing ranges and clubhouse facilities designed for both shooting and leisure. The property offers scenic Hill Country views, whether you are enjoying the course or socializing with friends.

Who oversees operations at the Horseshoe Bay Sporting Club? expand_more

Rhythm Outdoors, an experienced sporting club operator, with Adam McCaw serving as General Manager.
